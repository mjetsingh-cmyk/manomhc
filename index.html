Meet the founder

Built by a doctor who has seen the queue from both sides.

“Too often I have seen a patient's outcome decided not by the illness, but by hours lost finding a bed, a doctor or a unit of blood. Manova exists to give those hours back.”

Dr. Manjeet Singh is a medical doctor and clinical researcher who trained in India and Ireland. He completed his MBBS at Pt. B.D. He also holds an Advanced Diploma in Medical Law from King's Inns, Dublin.

His clinical work spans emergency triage and resuscitation during internship, medical-officer duties at a superspeciality hospital, and surgical house-officer work at a government medical college. FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Manova free for patients?

Yes. Creating an account, searching hospitals, checking availability and requesting care through Manova is free. You pay the hospital or provider directly for the treatment or service you receive.

Do I need an account to book?

No. You can send a request as a guest. A free account lets you track all your requests, save your health profile and emergency contacts, and book for family members.

How quickly will someone contact me?

Our care team responds within 24 hours, and usually much sooner for blood and home-care requests. For any emergency, always call 112 or 108 first.

Is my health information safe?

Your data is sent over an encrypted connection and used only to coordinate your care. You can download or delete your account data at any time from your dashboard. Read our Privacy Policy.

Can Manova help with Ayushman Bharat or insurance?

Yes. We help you check eligibility for government schemes like PM-JAY, CGHS, ECHS and ESIC, find empanelled or cashless hospitals, and prepare documents for pre-authorisation.

How does my hospital join Manova?

Fill the hospital registration form above. Our team verifies your details and sets up your dashboard within 24–48 hours. Government hospitals join free of charge.
